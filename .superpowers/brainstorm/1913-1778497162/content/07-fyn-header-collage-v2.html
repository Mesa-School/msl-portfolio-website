D1 — Diagonal Split (master + product)
Composition: 5:2 banner. Left ~58% = master (FYNERGY hands+drinks). Right ~42% = premium product shot (Hydra packaging). Diagonal slash separates them.

D1 · Diagonal Split (Brand × Product)

Master image dominates left, product hero "cuts in" from the right via a clean diagonal. Two blue palettes (sky blue + studio dark) create natural color tension. Most editorial.

Pros: single 5:2 footprint · brand wordmark stays prominent · product gets equal credit · most "designed"

D2 — Diagonal Cross (master × team)
Composition: 5:2 banner cut by a single diagonal. Top-left = brand master. Bottom-right = the 5-person team photo. "Brand vs People" duality.

D2 · Diagonal Cross (Brand × People)

Single diagonal cut, true half-and-half. Top-left = polished brand. Bottom-right = the actual humans behind it. Tells the "polished outside × real people inside" story in one frame.

Pros: most literal "half and half cross diagonally" · narrative is clear · works for every startup (master + team always exist)

D3 — Master + Polaroid Strip
Composition: 5:2 banner unchanged. 3 small "polaroids" tilted in the bottom-right: product, founder pitching, team. Adds depth without breaking the master.

D3 · Master + Polaroid Strip

Master banner stays untouched. 3 tilted polaroids float in the bottom-right corner — product, founder on stage, team. Playful, founder-personal, less "designed-by-committee."

Pros: banner stays sacred · polaroids show "real" moments · easy to swap photos per startup

My recommendation: D2 (Diagonal Cross). Most literal interpretation of your "half and half cross diagonally" idea, and the brand-vs-team duality is the most powerful story for an accelerator portfolio (we're showing investors both the polish AND the people). D1 is strong if you want to emphasize the product. D3 if you want the most casual / human energy.

Implementation note: all three use CSS clip-path for the diagonals — fully responsive, no image baking required. Per-startup config in JSON: header_collage: { master: "...", secondary: "...", layout: "diagonal-cross" }.
